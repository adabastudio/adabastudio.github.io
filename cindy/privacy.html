Privacy Policy

Cindy · Last updated 17 October 2026

Cindy is a workout app made by Adaba Studio. It is designed to work entirely on your iPhone. We run no server for it, and we don't collect, store, or share your personal data. This policy explains what the app keeps, and where.

What we do not do

  • We do not ask for an account, an email address, or a password.
  • We do not use analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs, and we serve no ads.
  • We do not track you across other apps or websites. Cindy shows no App Tracking Transparency prompt because it has nothing to ask for.
  • We do not access your camera, location, contacts, or health data.
  • We do not sell or share your data with anyone.

What the app stores

  • Your profile: the weekly session goal and the starting round goal you choose during onboarding.
  • Your workout history: the date, start and end time, and round count of each workout you complete.

This data is stored only on your device, in the app's local storage. It is never sent to us or to any third party. We hold no copy, so deleting the app deletes it for good.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, Cindy schedules reminders about your weekly goal locally on your device, timed from your own workout history. No notification data leaves your iPhone, and there is no push notification server. You can turn reminders off at any time in Settings › Notifications › Cindy.

Sharing a workout

After a workout you can share an image of your result. It is created on your device and only goes where you send it, through the standard iOS share sheet. We never see it.

Children

Cindy doesn't collect any information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes (for example, if a future version adds a feature that needs data to leave your device), the updated policy will be posted here with a new date before that version is released.
