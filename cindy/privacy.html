Privacy Policy

Cindy · Last updated 27 October 2026

Cindy is a workout app made by Adaba Studio. It is designed to work on your iPhone: your profile and workout history stay on your device. The only thing that leaves it is anonymous usage statistics, described below, which help us understand how the app is used and improve it. This policy explains what the app keeps, what it sends, and where.

What we do not do

  • We do not ask for an account, an email address, or a password.
  • We do not use advertising or tracking SDKs, and we serve no ads.
  • We do not track you across other apps or websites. Cindy shows no App Tracking Transparency prompt because it has nothing to ask for.
  • We do not access your camera, location, contacts, or health data.
  • We do not sell or share your data with anyone.

What the app stores

  • Your profile: the weekly session goal and the starting round goal you choose during onboarding.
  • Your workout history: the date, start and end time, and round count of each workout you complete.

This data is stored only on your device, in the app's local storage. It is never sent to us or to any third party. We hold no copy, so deleting the app deletes it for good.

Anonymous usage statistics

Starting with version 1.0.1, Cindy sends anonymous usage events to PostHog, an analytics service, hosted in the European Union (Frankfurt). For example: an onboarding step was viewed, a workout was started, finished or discarded, with its round count and duration, or the share sheet was opened.

Along with these events, PostHog receives technical details about the app and device: app version, iOS version, device model, language, whether reminders are allowed, and a random identifier generated on your device. That identifier isn't linked to your name, email, Apple ID, or any other app, and we never try to find out who you are. Your IP address isn't stored.

We use this data only to measure and improve Cindy. It is never sold, used for advertising, or used to track you across other apps and websites. It is kept for at most one year, then deleted. Deleting the app deletes the random identifier, after which new events can't be connected to the old ones.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, Cindy schedules reminders about your weekly goal locally on your device, timed from your own workout history. No notification data leaves your iPhone, and there is no push notification server. You can turn reminders off at any time in Settings › Notifications › Cindy.

Sharing a workout

After a workout you can share an image of your result. It is created on your device and only goes where you send it, through the standard iOS share sheet. We never see it: the usage statistics only note that the share sheet was opened.

Children

Cindy doesn't knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children. The anonymous usage statistics above don't identify anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes (for example, if a future version adds a feature that needs data to leave your device), the updated policy will be posted here with a new date before that version is released.
