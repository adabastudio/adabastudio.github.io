What Cindy is

Cindy is a workout tracker built around one fixed bodyweight routine: as many rounds as possible in 20 minutes of 5 pull-ups, 10 push-ups and 15 squats. It suggests a round goal based on your history, keeps count while you train, and tracks your weekly streak. Cindy is free and has no in-app purchases.

  • Round goals are suggestions. You decide how hard to push; train within your limits.
