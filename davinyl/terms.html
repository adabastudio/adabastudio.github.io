What Davinyl is

Davinyl is a companion app for managing a vinyl record collection. It works with Discogs, which supplies the record database and the price information. Davinyl is an independent app and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Discogs. A free Discogs account is required.

Free and Premium

Davinyl is free to download. The free tier includes a daily allowance of record scans with their Discogs price, the duplicate check across your entire collection on every scan, browsing your most recent records, one full valuation of your collection, the daily record pick, and a free JSON export of your data. Davinyl Premium unlocks unlimited scans, unlimited browsing, value history and the chart, your most valuable records, re-valuing on demand, and sorting by value. The exact limits are shown in the app under Settings → Your plan.

You are responsible for what you add to and remove from Discogs through Davinyl. You can export a full copy of your data at any time, for free, from Settings. Valuations are estimates

Values shown in Davinyl are derived from Discogs marketplace data and are estimates, not appraisals. They depend on condition, which Davinyl assumes to be VG+ unless you set it, and they change as the market changes. Do not rely on them for insurance, sale, or tax purposes without independent verification.
